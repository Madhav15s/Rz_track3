# ABSTENTION QUALITY AUDIT

Evaluating the financial value of the Support-Aware policy's ability to abstain (fall back to DO_NOTHING) when epistemic uncertainty is too high.

Evaluated on SEVERE_POSITIVITY regime, Seed 42.

- **Total Abstention Cases**: 14
- **Total Regret Avoided (Value Saved)**: -835,201 paise

When the Support-Aware model abstains, it prevents the underlying causal estimator (T-Learner) from executing out-of-support counterfactuals. This directly translates to saved intervention costs and avoided friction/risk penalties on customers who would not have responded.
