# SIMULATOR EQUATIONS AUDIT

## A. Latent (Hidden) Variables
The simulator generates exactly 11 latent variables per customer from beta and lognormal distributions:
1. `latent_payment_reliability` ~ Beta(6, 2)
2. `latent_liquidity` ~ Beta(3, 3)
3. `latent_digital_engagement` ~ Beta(2, 3)
4. `latent_price_sensitivity` ~ Beta(2, 5)
5. `latent_urgency` ~ Beta(2, 4)
6. `latent_customer_value` ~ Lognormal(10, 1.5)
7. `latent_retry_responsiveness` ~ Beta(2, 4)
8. `latent_delayed_retry_responsiveness` ~ Beta(3, 3)
9. `latent_message_responsiveness` ~ `latent_digital_engagement` * Beta(3, 2)
10. `latent_alternate_method_responsiveness` ~ `latent_liquidity` * Beta(2, 2)
11. `latent_human_escalation_responsiveness` ~ Beta(4, 2) (if value > 50k) else Beta(1, 5)

## B. Observable Features
These are the variables provided to the models. They are NOISY proxies of the latents or independently sampled:
1. `historical_payment_success_rate` = clip(`latent_payment_reliability` + N(0, 0.1), 0, 1)
2. `customer_value_band` = 'high' (value > 200k), 'medium', or 'low' (value < 50k)
3. `engagement_score` = clip(`latent_digital_engagement` + N(0, 0.1), 0, 1)
4. `payment_method` ~ Categorical(upi=0.6, card=0.3, netbanking=0.1)
5. `failure_code` ~ Categorical (probs depend on `payment_method`)
6. `amount_paise` = clip(Lognormal(7, 1.2) * 100, 1000, 10000000)
7. `time_since_failure_minutes` ~ Exponential(30)

## C. Base Natural Recovery Equation (p0)
The base probability of recovery with no intervention is calculated using a logit link function strictly on **HIDDEN LATENT VARIABLES** and the observable `failure_code`.

$$ \text{logit}(p_0) = -2.0 + 3.0 \times \text{latent\_payment\_reliability} + 2.0 \times \text{latent\_liquidity} - 1.0 \times I(\text{failure} = \text{INSUFFICIENT\_FUNDS}) - 1.5 \times I(\text{failure} = \text{BANK\_DECLINE}) $$

$$ p_0 = \frac{1}{1 + e^{-\text{logit}(p_0)}} $$

## D. Action-Specific Outcome Equations
Treatment outcomes are generated by applying additive latent-driven shifts to `logit(p0)`.

**Action 1: RETRY_NOW**
$$ \text{logit}(p_1) = \text{logit}(p_0) + 2.0 \times \text{latent\_retry\_responsiveness} - 2.0 \times I(\text{failure} = \text{INSUFFICIENT\_FUNDS}) + 1.5 \times I(\text{failure} = \text{BANK\_TIMEOUT}) $$

**Action 2: RETRY_LATER**
$$ \text{logit}(p_2) = \text{logit}(p_0) + 2.5 \times \text{latent\_delayed\_retry\_responsiveness} + 1.5 \times I(\text{failure} = \text{INSUFFICIENT\_FUNDS}) $$

**Action 3: PAYMENT_MESSAGE**
$$ \text{logit}(p_3) = \text{logit}(p_0) + 2.0 \times \text{latent\_message\_responsiveness} $$

**Action 4: ALTERNATE_METHOD**
$$ \text{logit}(p_4) = \text{logit}(p_0) + 3.0 \times \text{latent\_alternate\_method\_responsiveness} - 1.0 \times I(\text{method} = \text{netbanking}) $$

**Action 5: HUMAN_ESCALATION**
$$ \text{logit}(p_5) = \text{logit}(p_0) + 4.0 \times \text{latent\_human\_escalation\_responsiveness} $$

The final binary potential outcomes $Y_a$ are sampled from a Bernoulli distribution with probability $p_a$:
$$ Y_a \sim \text{Bernoulli}(p_a) $$

## E. Treatment Effect Equations
Because the link function is logistic, the true additive treatment effect on the probability scale is:
$$ \tau_a = \frac{1}{1 + e^{-\text{logit}(p_a)}} - \frac{1}{1 + e^{-\text{logit}(p_0)}} $$

## F. Which variables are hidden?
ALL latent variables listed in section A (reliability, liquidity, engagement, price_sens, urgency, value, and the 5 responsiveness scores). The true potential outcome probabilities ($p_0 \dots p_5$) are completely hidden.

## G. Which variables are observable?
ONLY the variables listed in section B (noisy success rate, band, noisy engagement, method, failure code, amount, time).

## H. Heterogeneous Treatment Interactions
Heterogeneity is injected directly into the data generating process through the latent interactions.
- `RETRY_NOW` has massive positive synergy with `BANK_TIMEOUT`, but massive negative penalty with `INSUFFICIENT_FUNDS`.
- `RETRY_LATER` specifically targets `INSUFFICIENT_FUNDS` with a positive synergy.
- `PAYMENT_MESSAGE` effectiveness is mathematically bounded by `latent_digital_engagement`.
- `ALTERNATE_METHOD` effectiveness is mathematically bounded by `latent_liquidity`, with a penalty if the original method was `netbanking`.

## Summary Correction
Previous reports stating that outcomes were a pure consequence of observable features were **FALSE**. The simulator relies heavily on unobserved latents. The models are succeeding by identifying the proxy relationships between the observables (like `historical_payment_success_rate`) and the true latent drivers (like `latent_payment_reliability`).
